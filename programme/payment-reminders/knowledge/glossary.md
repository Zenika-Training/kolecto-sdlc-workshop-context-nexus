# Glossary

- **Invoice due date**: the date by which the customer must pay.
- **Overdue**: unpaid after its due date.
- **Outstanding**: what is still to be paid on an invoice, after partial payments.
- **Reminder** (relance): a message asking the customer to pay an overdue invoice.
- **Step**: the reminder's tone, from friendly to formal notice (mise en demeure).
- **Recovery indemnity** (indemnité forfaitaire pour frais de recouvrement): a fixed amount the creditor may claim from a late business payer.
- **Late interest** (pénalités de retard): interest due on the outstanding amount for each day of delay.
- **DSO** (days sales outstanding): the average number of days customers take to pay.
- **Opt-out**: a customer who asked not to receive reminders.
- **Disputed invoice**: the customer contests the invoice; no reminder until resolved.
